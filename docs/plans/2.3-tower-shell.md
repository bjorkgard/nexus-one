### Landscape (WebSearch; Aside not installed)

- **Layer 1:** draw opaque first, transparent after with `depthWrite: false`, sort per object by centre ([three.js forum](https://discourse.threejs.org/t/threejs-and-the-transparent-problem/11553)). Three.js sorts objects, not triangles, so self-overlap inside one mesh is unsorted ([forum](https://discourse.threejs.org/t/how-to-solve-the-rendering-order-for-random-meshes-to-get-proper-transparence/64164.md)).
- **Layer 2:** weighted blended OIT exists as `OITPassNode` (WebGPU/TSL path, [three.js docs](https://threejs.org/docs/pages/OITPassNode.html)) and `three-wboit` (last pushed 2023, [GitHub](https://www.github.com/stevinz/three-wboit)). Alpha hashing trades sorting for grain.
- **Layer 3 (eureka):** the camera is orthographic and the tower is a convex box. With outward-facing, single-sided glass, only the walls facing the camera render; the far walls are back faces and are culled. A ray enters a convex hull once, so each pixel meets at most one front-facing glass face across the whole tower, at any yaw or pitch (so it carries into 3.1). Glass-over-glass sorting cannot occur. No OIT pass and no glass-vs-glass sort is needed. Inference, to be proven by the four screenshots plus a unit test on winding-derived face normals (cross product of each triangle in world space; three culls by winding, not the `normal` attribute). **Preconditions (spec review F1-F3):** glass is open planes on the hull with no thickness and no top/bottom faces, `side: FrontSide`; the other transparent object, the drei Grid (`transparent`, y = `LOOK.gridLift` 0.01), must not draw over glass (pending I2).
