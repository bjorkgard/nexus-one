### Landscape (WebSearch; Aside not installed)

- [Layer 1] Squarified treemap partition plus a corridor pass is the common procedural floor-plan method ([Mirahmadi & Shami 2012](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/1211.5842); [Lopes et al., TU Delft](https://graphics.tudelft.nl/~rafa/myPapers/bidarra.GAMEON10.pdf)).
- [Layer 2] Recent work treats BSP as a layout representation, not a generator ([HypergraphFormer 2026](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.18932)). No office-specific core-and-corridor generator found.
- [Layer 3] Real office towers use a centre-core plan: core, ring corridor, perimeter offices. A fixed ring template with seeded room splits along the perimeter band is simpler than a treemap, reads clearly from the iso camera, and makes corridor connectivity true by construction (which 2.9 needs). Generic treemap + corridor repair solves a problem we do not have (unknown room adjacency).

### 0A. Premise challenge

- Real problem: later steps need to know where things are. 2.5 needs desk slots and per-floor capacity; 2.6 needs core shafts; 2.9 needs walls, doors and corridors; 4.17 needs the ticker wall; 5.x zone view needs rooms the registry can resolve. Viewers need the building to read as offices, not empty glass trays.
- Plan solves the proxy (rooms as labelled boxes) directly, but leaves out walls/doors (2.9 blocks without them) and desk capacity (2.5 floor growth blocks without it).
- Do-nothing cost: 2.5 invents desk positions with no rooms; 2.9 has nothing to test "no edge crosses a wall" against.

### Working plan (accepted scope)

1. **Seeded PRNG** `src/world/rng.ts`: small deterministic generator plus a string/int hash for per-floor seeds. Shared with 2.5 and 2.7 later.
2. **Layout generator** `src/world/layout.ts` (A1): `buildLayout({ floors, seed })` returns, per floor, rooms (as zone inputs), walls, doors, anchors and desk slots. Centre-core ring template: core at the centre (elevator bank + stairwell footprints for 2.6), a ring corridor split into four AABB corridor rooms, and the perimeter band split by seeded widths into offices, meeting rooms and restrooms. Each floor's PRNG is seeded from `(seed, floor)` so one floor's layout does not depend on the floor count, except where F1 (pending) says otherwise.
3. **Rooms in the registry** (A1): `createZoneRegistry` takes the generated rooms as `kind: "room"` zones under `floor-N`, with a room type field; `validateZones` keeps checking overlap and parent containment. The break room (floor 1) and server room (top office floor) are produced by the generator.
4. **Lobby (floor 0):** reception room with reception desk anchor, waiting area with chair anchors, escalator footprint to `floor-1` (P6), cost-ticker wall anchor (content is 4.17, P12), core and restrooms.
5. **E1 walls + doors:** wall AABBs per room edge with door gaps onto the corridor ring; exported per floor for 2.9.
6. **E2 desk slots:** each office lists desk-cluster slots (position, facing) clear of doors; `deskCapacity(floor)` and a tower total for 2.5's floor growth.
7. **E3 interior rendering:** instanced low partitions and an open core frame with door gaps (O1) from the E1/A1 data, plus a per-room-type floor tint; existing palette materials only, interior parts `receiveShadow=false` (prior learning), one InstancedMesh per part kind via the `TowerPart` pattern.
8. **E4 lobby furniture:** instanced reception desk and waiting chairs in `palette.furniture` at the step-4 anchors.
9. **E5 label filter:** dev-only `?floor=N` (strict parser in `src/world/Stage.tsx`) limits room labels and room edges in `ZoneOverlay` to floor N.
10. **Tests:** unit (every floor has a core; rooms do not overlap; same seed same layout; every required room type exists; walls/doors/desk-slot invariants) and e2e screenshots labelling each room type.

Pending (not yet approved): F1 server-room stability when a floor is added (if floor T loses its server room, its rooms and `deskCapacity` change, breaking 2.5 desk stability); template sizes vs the existing break/server footprints; F2 escalator spanning two floor bands; room type field shape; core block height (visual, design review).
